\section{About}

I am an M.Sc.\ student at \textbf{Peking University}, advised by
Prof.~Wenjia Zhang and Prof.~Guanhua Chen, with a B.E.\ from \textbf{SUSTech}.
I am currently a \textbf{Researcher (Top Talent Program) at Meituan LongCat}.
Previously, I was mentored by Dr.~Li Dong at Microsoft Research Asia, GenAI Group.
I also serve as an \textbf{Area Chair for EMNLP 2026}.

\vspace{5pt}
My research focuses on \textbf{data synthesis and selection}
(FANNO, Tag-Instruct, InstructDiff, AlignDiff), \textbf{continual learning}
(ASFT, CFT), and \textbf{agent harnesses}
(LongCat-DeepResearch, UrbanClaw, and systems built around PlanGPT / PlanGPT-VL).
