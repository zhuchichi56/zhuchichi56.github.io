About

I am He Zhu (Zhuchichi), an M.Phil. student in Smart City & Big Data at Peking University, advised by Prof. Wenjia Zhang. I study large language models for urban intelligence, spanning instruction data synthesis, planning-focused LLMs, and spatial reasoning. I currently intern at Microsoft Research Asia (GENAI, Prof. Li Dong) and lead the PlanGPT series that enables planning institutes to co-design cities with AI.

Previously, I completed my B.E. in Computer Science at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) with a 90.2/100 GPA (top 10%) under Prof. Xuan Song. I have collaborated with SenseTime, Shanghai AI Lab, LocationMind Tokyo, the SUSTech-NLP group, UTokyo CSIS, and NUS. I also secured offers from Tencent QingYun and multiple top AI labs.

Projects

I am the first author and technical lead of PlanGPT, the first systematic study of LLMs for urban planning. The suite includes:

Learn more: plangpt.github.io

  • PlanGPT — Text LLM for planning scheme generation, powering Urban-Thinking, CoPlanner, and AuditPlanner.
  • PlanGPT-VL — Vision-language model for planning maps with hallucination control and hybrid training. Outperforms larger general VLMs on PlanBench-V.
  • PlanGPT-R1 — Reasoning-enhanced model for long-form planning documents.
  • UP-Bench & PlanBench-V — Benchmarks covering text, vision, and multimodal planning tasks.
