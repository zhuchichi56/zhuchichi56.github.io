PlanGPT Series

First comprehensive exploration of LLMs for end-to-end urban planning. I serve as first author and technical lead, coordinating a multi-institution team to ship research and production systems.

Core Models

  • PlanGPT: Text LLM for planning scheme generation with controllable design intents.
  • PlanGPT-VL: First planning-specialized vision-language model with hallucination control and hybrid training for map comprehension.
  • PlanGPT-R1: Reasoning-enhanced model for digesting dense planning documents.
