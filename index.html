About Me

I am a graduate student at Peking University, advised by Prof. Wenjia Zhang. My research focuses on Large Language Models and Urban Intelligence, aiming to develop intelligent systems that integrate spatial reasoning, vision-language understanding, and urban planning expertise.

Previously, I received my B.E. in Computer Science from Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), advised by Prof. Xuan Song. I have worked with institutions including Microsoft Research Asia, Shanghai AI Lab, and the University of Tokyo.

My research areas currently include: (1) Large Language Model, (2) Code Intelligence, (3) Urban Planning AI.
